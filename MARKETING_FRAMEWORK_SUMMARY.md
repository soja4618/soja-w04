# Marketing operating reference (high-level)

This is an original, non-substitutive summary of public marketing principles associated with Acquisition.com’s lead-generation material. It is not a reproduction of any book or course.

## SOJA’s marketing loop

1. Pick one named buyer and one painful, specific problem.
2. Create useful proof-led content that demonstrates the problem and the practical result, rather than vague promotion.
3. Choose a channel where that buyer already looks for solutions; contribute context before sharing an offer.
4. Use one clear call to action that points to the $19 Stripe offer.
5. Track qualified impressions, clicks, conversations, checkout starts, paid purchases, and repeat demand separately.
6. Keep or expand a channel only when it produces a measurable signal; stop low-signal experiments quickly.

## Guardrails

No spam, unsolicited mass messaging, fake testimonials, fabricated scarcity, paid promotion without approval, or counting reach as revenue. Marketing claims must be supportable by the product and verified evidence.

## Current application

For Evidence Manifest Template Pack, test developer/QA/agent-operator audiences with short demonstrations of a verification edge case, a concise explanation of time saved, and one Stripe CTA. Keep the public storefront premium and consistent; use GitHub/Netlify as the source and distribution surface, not multiple drifting copies.
