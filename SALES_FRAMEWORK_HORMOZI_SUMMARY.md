# Offer-design reference: $100M Offers (high-level)

This is a brief, original operating summary based on public material from Acquisition.com, not a reproduction of the book.

## Principles SOJA may apply

- Start with a narrow buyer and a specific painful problem; do not market to everyone.
- Define the desired outcome in the buyer's language and make the path to it concrete.
- Increase perceived value by improving the likely result and reducing waiting, uncertainty, and effort.
- Bundle a focused core deliverable with useful implementation guidance, examples, or checks when they materially improve the outcome.
- Add proof and specificity before adding hype: show what is included, who it is for, what changes after purchase, and what the buyer receives.
- Use a clear price, risk-reducing explanation, and one obvious next action.
- Test demand and conversion evidence before expanding the product or adding complexity.

## SOJA application

For the Evidence Manifest Template Pack, lead with the buyer outcome (faster, more reliable verification tests), show the five concrete edge cases, explain the synthetic-data boundary, keep the $19 one-time price visible, and make the Stripe checkout the primary call to action. Treat design, copy, and distribution experiments as hypotheses; count only paid purchases as revenue.
