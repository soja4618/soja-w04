## Funnel ownership

1. Netlify earns attention and explains the value.
2. Every Evidence Manifest purchase CTA routes to the canonical Gumroad product.
3. Gumroad handles checkout, receipt, and file delivery.
4. SOJA verifies public availability and records only aggregate, non-sensitive evidence that is legitimately available.
5. Obsidian records current state, experiments, outcomes, and the next ranked action.

## Change control

Payment destinations, payouts, banking/tax, security, recovery, ownership, and account permissions remain owner-reserved. SOJA has standing authorization to improve public copy, product files, listings, fulfillment contents, price and packaging, and routine deployments on existing SOJA commercial accounts when the change is truthful, platform-compliant, evidence-backed, reversible, recorded before execution, and independently verified afterward. Gumroad remains the canonical checkout and gated fulfillment path unless the owner explicitly changes that architecture.
